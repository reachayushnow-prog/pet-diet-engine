Fromm Dog Food Recall 2026: Metal Contamination & Affected Products

A current recall affects specific lots of two Fromm canned dog food products because of potential metal contamination. Here is how to identify the affected cans, what the official notices say, and what dog owners should do next.

Published: August 24, 2026 Updated: August 24, 2026 Source checked: FDA & Fromm Family Foods Category: Pet Food Safety
⚠️ Recall alert The current August 2026 recall concerns specific lots of Fromm Turkey Pâté Wet Dog Food and Fromm Diner Classics Milo's Meatloaf Pâté Wet Dog Food because of potential metal contamination. Do not assume that every Fromm product is affected.

Is Fromm dog food recalled in 2026?

Yes. On August 21, 2026, Fromm Family Foods announced a voluntary recall involving specific lots of two canned dog food products. The U.S. Food and Drug Administration published the company announcement the same day.

The stated reason for the recall is potential metal contamination. The affected products were distributed through neighborhood pet stores and online outlets in the United States and Canada.

The most important point for pet owners is that the recall is identified by the product name, can size, UPC, lot code and Best By date. Checking those details is more reliable than simply searching for the Fromm brand name.

Why was Fromm dog food recalled?

The recall was initiated after complaints involving potential metal contamination. Fromm stated that it identified the error and implemented corrective actions in addition to its existing safety processes.

Metal contamination is a concern because physical material can potentially injure the mouth or gastrointestinal tract. Depending on the size and amount involved, ingestion may also create a risk of gastrointestinal obstruction.

The FDA notice lists possible symptoms that may occur after ingestion of metal, including choking, vomiting, loss of appetite, lethargy and stomach discomfort.

What should you do if you have recalled Fromm dog food?

Recommended next steps

  • Stop feeding the affected product.
  • Keep the can or packaging available so you can verify the product information.
  • Return the recalled product to the retailer where it was purchased.
  • If your dog consumed the affected product and develops concerning symptoms, contact your veterinarian.
  • For recall-specific questions, use the contact information provided by Fromm Family Foods.

Do not intentionally feed a recalled product simply because your dog appears normal. A product recall means the affected product should no longer be fed.

What symptoms should dog owners watch for?

The FDA and Fromm recall notice identifies several possible symptoms associated with ingestion of metal-contaminated food.

Choking Possible difficulty caused by a foreign object.
Vomiting A possible gastrointestinal response.
Loss of appetite A change in normal eating behavior.
Lethargy Unusual tiredness or reduced activity.
Stomach discomfort Signs of gastrointestinal distress.
Other concerning changes Any unusual or worsening symptoms deserve professional attention.

These signs are not specific to metal exposure and can have many causes. If your dog consumed recalled food and becomes unwell, your veterinarian is the appropriate source for individual medical advice.

Have illnesses or injuries been reported?

As of the August 21, 2026 recall announcement, the FDA notice states that there had been no reported illnesses or injuries related to the recalled products.

That statement reflects the information available when the recall was announced. Pet owners should continue to check the official FDA and Fromm notices for any future updates.

Where was the recalled Fromm dog food sold?

According to the recall announcement, the affected products were distributed through neighborhood pet stores and online outlets in the United States and Canada.

If you purchased Fromm canned dog food online, check the product information on the can or packaging rather than assuming that an online listing is current.

Are all Fromm dog foods recalled?

No. The current notice does not mean that every Fromm dog food product has been recalled.

The August 21, 2026 notice specifically identifies the two canned products listed in this article and states that no other Fromm products are affected by this recall.

This distinction matters because searching for “Fromm dog food recall” may produce broad results that do not tell you whether a particular can is included. Product-level identification is the safer approach.
