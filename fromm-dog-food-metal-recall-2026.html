Fromm Dog Food Metal Recall 2026: Affected Products, Lot Codes & What to Do

A practical guide for dog owners checking whether their Fromm canned dog food is included in the August 2026 recall involving potential metal contamination.

Last reviewed: August 24, 2026. Always verify current recall information with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration and the manufacturer because recall information can change.

What happened with the Fromm dog food recall?

Fromm Family Foods announced a voluntary recall on August 21, 2026 involving two specific canned dog food products because of potential metal contamination. The U.S. Food and Drug Administration published the recall announcement as part of its animal and veterinary safety information.

This is an important distinction for dog owners: the recall does not mean that every Fromm product has been recalled. The current notice identifies two specific canned dog foods and their corresponding product identification information.

If you have one of the affected products: Stop feeding it to your dog and return the recalled product to the retailer.

According to the recall notice, the affected products were distributed through neighborhood pet stores and online outlets in the United States and Canada.

Which Fromm dog food products are recalled?

The safest way to check your product is to compare the name, package size, UPC, lot code and Best By information on the can with the information below.

Product Size UPC Lot Code Best By
Fromm Turkey Pâté Wet Dog Food 12.2 oz. per can 072705118700 EP2A3306 551006 032029
Fromm Diner Classics Milo's Meatloaf Pâté Wet Dog Food 12.5 oz. per can 072705132324 EP2A3306 551029 032029

Compare the complete information rather than relying only on the product name. A product with a similar name is not automatically part of this recall.

Why was Fromm dog food recalled?

The stated reason for the recall is potential metal contamination. The recall notice says the company initiated the recall after receiving complaints involving metal contamination.

Metal contamination can be particularly concerning if sharp material is present because it can potentially injure the mouth or gastrointestinal tract. Larger amounts can also create a risk of gastrointestinal obstruction.

The FDA notice reports that, as of the announcement, there had been no reported illnesses or injuries associated with the recalled products.

Important: A recall is a precautionary food-safety action. Do not assume that a dog is affected simply because it ate Fromm food. First determine whether the exact product and identification information match the recall.

How to check your Fromm dog food at home

If you currently have Fromm canned dog food, take a few minutes to inspect the packaging before feeding it.

1. Identify the product name.
Check whether it is Fromm Turkey Pâté or Fromm Diner Classics Milo's Meatloaf Pâté.
2. Check the can size.
Compare the net weight with the recalled size listed above.
3. Find the UPC.
Compare the UPC printed on the product with the recall information.
4. Find the lot code.
Compare the complete lot code, including all characters and numbers.
5. Check the Best By information.
The affected products listed in the recall have Best By information of 032029.

If all the relevant identifiers match the recalled product information, do not feed the product to your dog.

What should you do if you have recalled Fromm dog food?

  1. Stop feeding the recalled product. Do not continue feeding it simply because your dog appears normal.
  2. Keep the product separate. Store it away from other pet food so it is not accidentally fed.
  3. Return it to the retailer. The recall notice advises consumers who purchased the affected products to return them to their retailer.
  4. Keep the product information. If you need to contact the manufacturer, having the package, lot code, UPC and purchase information can make the conversation easier.
  5. Monitor your dog. If your dog consumed the affected product and develops concerning symptoms, contact your veterinarian promptly.
Symptoms mentioned in the recall notice include:
  • Choking
  • Vomiting
  • Loss of appetite
  • Lethargy
  • Stomach discomfort

These symptoms can have many possible causes. A veterinarian is the appropriate person to assess your dog's condition.

Are all Fromm dog foods recalled?

No. The current recall notice specifically identifies the two canned dog food products listed in this article.

The FDA-published announcement states that there are no other Fromm products affected by this recall.

This is why dog owners should avoid broad statements such as "all Fromm dog food is recalled." The correct approach is to identify the exact product and lot information.

What if my dog ate the recalled food but seems fine?

Do not panic, but do not ignore the recall either.

First confirm whether the food your dog ate matches the recalled product, UPC and lot information. If it does, stop feeding that product.

The FDA recall notice states that there were no reported illnesses or injuries associated with the recalled products at the time of the announcement. However, the notice also explains that swallowed metal can potentially cause serious problems.

If your dog consumed affected food and you are concerned about possible ingestion of metal, contact your veterinarian for individualized advice. Seek urgent veterinary attention if your dog develops serious or rapidly worsening symptoms.

How to choose a replacement dog food after a recall

A food recall can make dog owners want to change diets immediately. However, choosing a replacement should be based on your dog's individual nutritional needs rather than simply choosing the first available product.

Consider your dog's age, body condition, activity level, existing medical needs and normal feeding pattern.

For healthy dogs, a complete and balanced commercial diet appropriate for the dog's life stage may be a practical starting point. Dogs with medical conditions or special dietary requirements may need a veterinarian's guidance before making a significant diet change.

Remember that calorie requirements can vary considerably between individual dogs even when they weigh the same amount.
