What Does the Pet Nutrition Quiz Cover?

The quiz covers general educational topics including pet food labels, protein, calories, ingredient safety, homemade feeding, hydration, digestive health and differences between dog and cat nutrition.

Dog and Cat Nutrition

Dogs and cats have different nutritional biology and should not automatically be treated as having identical dietary requirements. Cats, for example, have specific nutrients that must be supplied through an appropriate diet.

Pet Food Safety

Responsible pet feeding also means recognizing potentially unsafe foods and understanding safe preparation, storage and handling practices.

Homemade Pet Food

Homemade meals require careful nutritional planning. Combining meat, vegetables and grains does not automatically create a complete and balanced long-term diet.

Calories and Body Condition

Calorie needs vary between individual animals. Weight, activity, age, life stage and body condition can influence how much energy a pet needs.
