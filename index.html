Guide: Transitioning Pets to Custom Diets

When adjusting your pet's dietary ratios, always transition gradually over a 7 to 10 day period to prevent digestive upset. Start by mixing 25% of the new diet with 75% of their old food, slowly increasing the new proportion over time.

For pets with chronic skin or digestive issues, consult a certified veterinary nutritionist to perform targeted elimination diets before adding single-protein toppers or home-cooked ingredients.
