1. Why Fresh Food Matters: Kibble vs. Whole-Food Diets

For decades, ultra-processed kibble has been the standard for pet nutrition due to its convenience and long shelf life.

The Proven Benefits of Whole-Food Diets

  • Enhanced Digestive Health: Fresh diets produce smaller, firmer, and less odorous stools.
  • Vibrant Skin and Coat: Whole proteins and natural essential fatty acids reduce itching and shedding.
  • Weight Management: Sustained energy without sudden blood sugar spikes.
  • Better Hydration: Fresh foods contain natural moisture, supporting kidney function.
